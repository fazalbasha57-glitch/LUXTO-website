PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 17 November 2026

LUXTO India Private Limited (“LUXTO”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

When you join our launch list using the “Notify Me” form, we collect:

  • Your email address, which you give us voluntarily.
  • Basic technical details: the page you signed up from, your browser and device type, and the date and time of signup.

If you email or call us, we also keep the details you share in that conversation.

2. Why we collect it

  • To send you a confirmation email when you sign up.
  • To tell you when LUXTO products launch, and to share important news about them.
  • To keep our website secure and prevent spam or misuse.

We rely on your consent to do this. We will not use your email for anything unrelated to LUXTO, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. Where your information is stored

Signup details are stored in Google Sheets and sent using Google services, which means they are processed on Google’s secure servers, which may be outside India. Access is limited to authorised LUXTO team members who need it for the purposes above.

4. Sharing

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run the website and send emails (such as Google), and when the law requires it. These providers may only use it to provide their service to us.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your details until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, or until they are no longer needed for the purposes in this policy, whichever comes first.

6. Your choices and rights

You can, at any time:

  • Unsubscribe by replying “unsubscribe” to any of our emails or writing to us.
  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your information.
  • Withdraw your consent, which will not affect anything done before you withdrew it.

Write to info@luxtoindia.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies. If we use an analytics tool, it collects anonymous information about how the site is used, such as pages viewed and the type of device, to help us improve it.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including limiting access and using trusted providers. No online service can be guaranteed completely secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

9. Children

This website is not intended for children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
